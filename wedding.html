Travel & Accommodations

Getting There

The wedding is in Tūja, a small coastal town about an hour from Riga. Guests should fly into Riga International Airport (RIX). Direct flights from Switzerland or the Netherlands are available via AirBaltic, Transavia, Lufthansa, LOT Airlines, and Air Dolomiti.

From the Airport

  • Taxi: Bolt app (approx. €10)
  • Public Transport: Bus tickets €1.50 via Mobilly app
  • Car Sharing: Bolt Drive, CityBee, CarGuru (approx. €25/day)

Accommodations

On June 27 and 28, we will provide shared or individual room accommodations at the venue, along with meals and transportation from/to Riga city center.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?
Dress formally, but no specific style is required. Women should avoid wearing white. Avoid thin or pointy heels due to beach/grass terrain. Bring a warm layer and possibly an umbrella!
What is the RSVP deadline?
Please RSVP by April 15, 2026.
Can I bring a plus one?
This is a small, intimate wedding; plus ones are only allowed if specifically mentioned on your invitation.
Do you have a gift registry?
We prefer not to receive large physical gifts. Contributions toward our honeymoon and new life together are warmly welcomed.
Will there be dietary options?
Yes. Please let us know by April 15, 2026, if you need vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free meals, or have allergies.
